# "Paste your URL, get an AI chatbot" - comparables (global + South Asia), as of 2026-11-24

All prices are what the vendor's live page showed when fetched on 2026-11-24, in USD unless stated. Where a page gave no date, that is noted. Tool budget was about 17 calls, so Gupshup, Yellow.ai and Haptik pricing, plus Voiceflow's numbers, were not checked (see Gaps).

## 1. Product-by-product: onboarding, limits, refresh, channels, handoff, API, white-label

### Takeaway
The self-serve "train on your website" group (Chatbase, SiteGPT, DocsBot, CustomGPT) all sell the same bundle: crawl or upload, a widget, Messenger/WhatsApp/Slack connectors, an API, and paid branding removal. They compete on page or storage limits, how often they re-crawl, and what they charge for credits. Helpdesk suites (Intercom Fin, Tidio Lyro, Crisp) sell the bot as part of a live-chat inbox. Sarvam sells voice agents billed per minute. Its product page does not mention a website text widget or website crawling.

### Cited findings
**Chatbase** (pricing page shows (c) 2026 and a G2 "Spring 2026" badge)
- Basic is $150/mo ($120/mo yearly) with 4,000 message credits. Business is $500/mo ($400/mo yearly) with 15,000 credits. Both include 1 agent and 40 MB of training content. Enterprise is custom. There is a 7-day trial. - [Chatbase pricing](https://www.chatbase.co/pricing)
- "Auto-resync agents" is included on every plan, but the page gives no frequency. WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram, Slack, voice/telephony and the API are included on all paid plans. - [Chatbase pricing](https://www.chatbase.co/pricing)
- Removing "Powered by Chatbase" is a $99/mo add-on, and full white-label is Enterprise only. Auto-recharge is $40 per 1,000 credits, and these credits don't expire. Extra agents are $25/mo each, and extra storage is $25 per 10 MB/mo. - [Chatbase pricing](https://www.chatbase.co/pricing)
- Note: these entry prices are far above Chatbase's earlier $19-$40 Hobby tier, and G2 snapshots still show $40 and $19 Hobby prices. Third-party sources disagree on the overage rate ($40 vs $14 per 1,000 credits). The live page is authoritative. - [search summary of featurebase/activepieces/G2](https://www.featurebase.app/blog/chatbase-pricing)

**CustomGPT.ai**
- Standard is $99/mo ($89 yearly): 1,000 queries/mo, 10 agents, 5,000 documents per agent, 60M words of storage, 3 seats. Premium is $499/mo ($449 yearly): 5,000 queries, 25 agents, 20,000 documents per agent, 300M words. Enterprise is custom. There is a 7-day trial. - [CustomGPT pricing](https://customgpt.ai/pricing/)
- Every plan includes citations and "anti-hallucination", with 90+ languages (the FAQ says 92+). Standard has a RAG API and an OpenAI-compatible API. Branding removal starts on Premium. Its claim of "#1 for anti-hallucination" is the vendor's own. - [CustomGPT pricing](https://customgpt.ai/pricing/)

**SiteGPT** (the clearest view of re-crawl frequency)
- Starter is $59/mo ($39 yearly): 1,000 pages, 1 bot, 4k messages, manual refresh only, no API. - [SiteGPT pricing](https://sitegpt.ai/pricing)
- Growth is $129/mo ($79 yearly): 10,000 pages, 2 bots, monthly auto-refresh, API, and Messenger/Slack/Google Chat/Crisp/Freshdesk/Zoho connectors. Handoff goes to an iOS live-inbox app with push alerts and "one-tap takeover". - [SiteGPT pricing](https://sitegpt.ai/pricing)
- Scale is $429/mo ($259 yearly): 50,000 pages, weekly refresh plus a daily sitemap scan, and Zendesk. Enterprise covers 500,000 pages with daily refresh. White-label is on request. Extra 5k messages cost $59/mo. There is a 7-day trial that needs a card. - [SiteGPT pricing](https://sitegpt.ai/pricing)

**DocsBot**
- Free covers 1 bot, 50 source pages and 100 credits. Personal is $49: 5k pages, 5k credits, monthly refresh. Standard is $149: 3 bots, 15k pages, weekly refresh. Business is $499: 10 bots, 100k pages, 60k credits, weekly refresh and unbranded widgets. Annual billing gives two months free. - [DocsBot pricing](https://docsbot.ai/pricing)
- Credits are 1 per message on the 1x model, and bigger models cost more. From Personal up, plans include Slack, Messenger/WhatsApp/Telegram, lead-collection forms, Calendly/Cal.com booking, Zapier and MCP. There is a public API. - [DocsBot pricing](https://docsbot.ai/pricing)

**Botpress** (builder platform, not paste-a-URL first)
- Free is 100 conversations/mo, 3 seats and 3 bots. Plus is $150/mo billed annually: 250 conversations, $25 of AI spend, and a white-label webchat. Team is $750/mo: 1,500 conversations and $150 of AI spend. Extra conversations cost $0.65 (Plus) or $0.50 (Team). Each conversation includes $0.10 of AI usage. AI credit is auto-bought in $10 grants at cost. Viber, Botpress Desk (human inbox) and a Help Center come with the plans. - [Botpress pricing](https://botpress.com/pricing)

**Voiceflow**
- The pricing page now publishes no numbers. It shows an Agencies & Partners track (free trial with no card, usage-based billing, multi-client workspaces, white-label, client handoff) and a Businesses track ("Request pricing / Book a demo"). It has moved upmarket. - [Voiceflow pricing](https://www.voiceflow.com/pricing)

**Intercom Fin**
- Fin costs $0.99 per outcome. It is sold standalone on top of Zendesk, Salesforce, HubSpot or Freshdesk with no seat fees, subject to a monthly minimum (the page's example is 50 outcomes). Intercom helpdesk seats cost $19 (Essential, a promo from $29), $85 (Advanced) and $132 (Expert) per seat/mo, billed annually. - [Intercom pricing](https://www.intercom.com/pricing)

**Tidio Lyro**
- Free gives 50 human conversations/mo plus 50 Lyro AI conversations once, which do not renew. Starter is $33/mo with 50 conversations. Growth starts at $63/mo with 100-1,000 conversations. Plus starts at $300/mo plus usage. Premium includes 3,000+ Lyro conversations, a guaranteed 50% Lyro resolution rate and pay-per-resolution billing. No per-resolution price is published. - [Tidio pricing](https://www.tidio.com/pricing/)

**Crisp**
- Free is 2 seats with no AI. Mini is $45/mo: 4 seats, $5 of AI credit, about 90 automated conversations. Essentials is $95/mo: 10 seats, $25 of AI credit, about 450 automated conversations. Plus is $295/mo: 20+ seats, $75 of AI credit, about 1,350 automated conversations. Extra agents are $10/mo. - [Crisp pricing](https://crisp.chat/en/pricing/)
- Channels include widget, email, Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp Business, Telegram, **Viber**, SMS, Line and phone. The page mentions "AI Knowledge training" but not website crawling. - [Crisp pricing](https://crisp.chat/en/pricing/)

**Wati Astra (India, WhatsApp-first)**
- Free gives 1 agent for website/WhatsApp (one version of the page says web widget only), 100 credits/mo and 1 MB of training data. Third parties report Pro at $99/mo (up to 3 agents, 5,000 credits, 50 MB per agent) and Business at $399/mo (25,000 credits, 100 MB). You train it by uploading website content, docs and FAQs. - [Wati Astra pricing](https://www.wati.io/products/astra/pricing/); [eesel review](https://www.eesel.ai/blog/wati-pricing)
- A Wati + Astra bundle is sold only in India. Its Growth plan has 1 Astra agent and 5 MB of training data, and AI credits are shared, with a minimum top-up of 2,000. - [Wati support](https://support.wati.io/en/articles/17565154-understanding-wati-and-astra-bundle-plans-and-ai-credits)
- A competitor (SleekFlow) says Astra is easy to deploy mainly as a website live-chat agent, with WhatsApp running through a separate Knowbot. This is a biased source. - [SleekFlow](https://sleekflow.io/blog/wati-astra-ai-review)

**Sarvam AI**
- The "conversational agents" page is the **Sarvam Voice Agents Platform**. It handles inbound and outbound phone calls, lets you rent a number or bring your own, links a knowledge base, and calls APIs during a call. "Samvaad" appears only in a "Build your own agent" link. The page does not mention a website widget, WhatsApp or website crawling. - [Sarvam conversational agents](https://www.sarvam.ai/products/conversational-agents)
- Pricing (ex-GST, per minute, models included): pay-as-you-go ₹3.50, Pro ₹3.25 (₹9,999 minimum), Growth ₹3.15 (₹49,999 minimum), Business ₹3.00 (₹1,99,999 minimum), and Enterprise custom from 2 lakh min/mo. WhatsApp campaigns are Enterprise only. Telephony costs ₹0.20 per 30s pulse, and a number costs ₹59 per 30 days. The page covers 11 Indian languages plus English, including code-mixed speech. - [Sarvam docs pricing](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/pricing)
- In Oct 2025 (updated Nov 2025), Samvaad voice and chat agents launched on WhatsApp in 11 Indian languages, with switching between voice and chat in one thread through Meta's Business Calling API. It is a no-code builder with templates for lead qualification, KYC, order capture, support and bookings. The article names no pricing and does not mention website crawling. - [AIM](https://analyticsindiamag.com/ai-news-updates/sarvams-samvaad-voice-chat-ai-agents-hit-whatsapp-in-11-indian-languages)
- Inc42 reported that Sarvam would open its agents platform to the public with free-tier and usage-based pricing. This is unconfirmed and may be dated. - [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/exclusive-sarvam-ai-to-open-voice-ai-agents-platform-for-public-use/)

### Inferences
- Sarvam is a voice-first, enterprise-leaning, per-minute product. It is not a "paste your URL, get a text widget" product. That leaves the self-serve, text-first, website-grounded slot open in Nepali.
- Refresh frequency is a common upsell lever: SiteGPT gates it as manual, monthly, weekly then daily, and DocsBot as none, monthly then weekly. Boli could make re-crawl a feature that varies by plan.
- Viber is uncommon among the global players. Only Crisp and Botpress list it. In Nepal, Viber matters, so this could set Boli apart.
- Branding removal is almost always paid (Chatbase $99/mo add-on, DocsBot Business tier, CustomGPT Premium, Botpress Plus).

### Gaps
- Gupshup, Yellow.ai and Haptik pricing and URL-crawl onboarding were not researched within the budget. All three are enterprise and sales-led.
- Voiceflow publishes no prices.
- Chatbase's page and character limits and its re-crawl frequency are not stated on the page (only 40 MB).
- Analytics features (unanswered or top questions), as shown on the plan pages, were not verified for each vendor.

## 2. Pricing models side by side

### Takeaway
There are four models: credits or messages per month (Chatbase, SiteGPT, DocsBot, CustomGPT, Wati), per conversation (Botpress, Tidio, Crisp's estimated automated conversations), per resolution (Fin $0.99, Tidio Premium), and per minute (Sarvam voice). Paid entry tiers range from about $39-$59/mo (SiteGPT, DocsBot) to $150/mo (Chatbase, Botpress).

### Cited findings
- Price per included message at list price: Chatbase Basic $150 / 4,000 = $0.0375. DocsBot Personal $49 / 5,000 = about $0.01 on the 1x model. SiteGPT Starter $59 / 4,000 = about $0.015. CustomGPT Standard $99 / 1,000 queries = $0.099. These are calculated from [Chatbase](https://www.chatbase.co/pricing), [DocsBot](https://docsbot.ai/pricing), [SiteGPT](https://sitegpt.ai/pricing) and [CustomGPT](https://customgpt.ai/pricing/).
- Per-conversation overage: Botpress $0.50-$0.65. - [Botpress](https://botpress.com/pricing)
- Per-resolution: Fin $0.99. - [Intercom](https://www.intercom.com/pricing)
- Free tiers: DocsBot (50 pages, 100 credits), Botpress (100 conversations), Tidio (50 Lyro conversations once), Wati Astra (100 credits, 1 MB), Crisp (no AI). Chatbase, SiteGPT and CustomGPT offer only 7-day trials. - sources above

### Inferences
- A Nepali SMB will not pay $150/mo. The useful reference point is the $39-$59/mo entry tiers and Wati's India-only bundle, which shows global vendors cutting prices for South Asia. NPR credit packs priced per message, with refresh frequency and branding removal as upsells, fit the market.

### Gaps
- No public price in NPR from any competitor. Haptik, Yellow.ai and Gupshup India rupee prices were not checked.

## 3. What users praise and complain about

### Takeaway
Complaints focus on billing and cost predictability (credit burn, $0.99 "assumed" resolutions), support and refunds, and disappearing training data. Core answer quality draws fewer complaints. Users praise fast setup.

### Cited findings
- Chatbase: G2 rating about 4.7 (13-14 reviews), Capterra 4.3 (73 reviews). Reviewers call it pricey once you need more than the basic plan, and messages "a bit expensive". One response can use 2-6 credits depending on the model. Negative reviews focus on billing, refunds, disappearing training data and slow support. A Trustpilot rating of about 2 stars was reported second-hand and not verified. - [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/p/10012414/Chatbase/reviews/); [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/chatbase-chatbase/reviews); [myaskai guide](https://myaskai.com/blog/chatbase-complete-guide-2026)
- Intercom Fin: G2 reviewers say per-resolution billing makes monthly cost hard to forecast. One asks for a flat monthly bundle. One says many "resolved" conversations are only "assumed resolved" and still cost $0.99. Others say it pays for itself compared with hiring. - [G2 Fin reviews](https://www.g2.com/products/fin-by-intercom/reviews)

### Inferences
- Boli's pitch should stress predictable prepaid NPR credits (no surprise bills, credits that don't expire like Chatbase's top-ups) and transparent "what we trained on" views, so training data never seems to vanish.

### Gaps
- No Reddit threads surfaced in the searches. Hallucination-specific complaints were not found in review snippets, which does not mean they don't exist.

## 4. Table stakes for v1 vs nice to have; onboarding demos

### Takeaway
At every price point the common floor is: URL crawl plus sitemap plus file upload, an embeddable widget with basic styling, an API, lead capture, a conversation log, and some form of refresh. Messenger and WhatsApp are included from the cheapest paid tier at Chatbase and DocsBot. Human handoff, CRM sync, booking, voice and MCP are differentiators.

### Cited findings
- Messenger/WhatsApp/Slack/API are on all Chatbase paid plans. Lead forms, booking and Messenger/WhatsApp/Telegram are on DocsBot from $49. Citations are on all CustomGPT plans. Refresh, API and a handoff inbox are gated to SiteGPT Growth. - [Chatbase](https://www.chatbase.co/pricing); [DocsBot](https://docsbot.ai/pricing); [CustomGPT](https://customgpt.ai/pricing/); [SiteGPT](https://sitegpt.ai/pricing)

### Inferences (my proposed v1 split for Boli)
- **v1 must have:** URL plus sitemap crawl with a page count and a list of ingested pages, PDF/doc upload, manual Q&A overrides, a widget (colour, logo, greeting, starter questions), source links on answers, an "I don't know, contact us" fallback with lead capture, a conversation log with unanswered questions, an API key, and manual re-crawl.
- **Next:** scheduled re-crawl, Messenger/Viber/WhatsApp, a human-handoff inbox, branding removal, and top-questions analytics.

### Gaps
- No source captured a measured "chatbot ready in 60 seconds" claim or time-to-first-answer benchmark.

## 5. Demo-led selling

### Takeaway
A recognised agency tactic is the "reverse demo": build a bot from the prospect's own website and send the link before the first call. Tools now automate it, but every conversion figure is a vendor claim. Chatbase's own growth was product-led (viral launch, self-serve).

### Cited findings
- Ciela AI takes a prospect's URL and generates a chat, voice or text-back agent with the prospect's logo, colours and font. The agency pastes the link into its outreach. The method has blog posts from Nov 2025, May 2026 and Aug 2026. - [Ciela reverse demo](https://ciela.ai/blogs/reverse-demo-method-ai-agencies); [Ciela 2026](https://ciela.ai/blogs/theo-demo-agent-reverse-demo-method)
- GrowwStacks (GoHighLevel) scrapes the prospect's site into a knowledge base for a personalised demo. It claims 3-5x higher conversion against a 1-3% cold-outreach baseline, with no methodology. - [GrowwStacks](https://growwstacks.com/blog/how-to-sell-ai-agents-with-personalized-demos)
- Chatbase growth: about $64K MRR within about 6 months of its 2023 launch, about $5M ARR around its second anniversary (Indie Hackers, undated), $8M ARR with 18 people (ProductLed), and $10M ARR in March 2026, bootstrapped and profitable (Stripe case study). - [Stripe](https://stripe.com/customers/chatbase); [Indie Hackers](https://www.indiehackers.com/post/TpbhTVyp1sjBk0uzo4tR); [ProductLed](https://productled.com/blog/how-chatbase-hit-8m-arr-with-18-people)

### Inferences
- For Boli, a "we already built your bot - try it in Nepali" link sent to Nepali SMBs (colleges, hotels, ISPs, clinics) is cheap to produce if crawling is automated. Build it from public pages only and do not publish it on the prospect's site without consent.

### Gaps
- No independent conversion data exists for pre-built demos. The Starter Story breakdown of Chatbase could not be fetched because the domain was blocked.

## 6. Nepal / South Asia players and Romanized Hindi or Nepali

### Takeaway
No Nepali self-serve "paste URL" product turned up. Local players are voice-outreach platforms (TingTing), consumer chat apps (Hajur AI) or agencies (Nepdev). Haptik is the clearest documented Hinglish (Romanized Hindi) case. Romanized Nepali support is barely documented anywhere.

### Cited findings
- TingTing (Nepal) has its own Nepali speech-to-text, text-to-speech and conversational AI built for voice outreach. It added multi-modal AI agents and voice forms around Jan 2026. - [ICT Frame](https://ictframe.com/?p=104410); [Gadgetbyte](https://www.gadgetbytenepal.com/tingting)
- Hajur AI is a Nepali-language consumer chat app launched in March 2026. - [eKantipur](https://ekantipur.com/technology/2026/03/03/en/hazur-ai-app-released-57-44.html)
- Springbase.AI (June 2026) is a single console that resells AI services to Nepali businesses. - [eKantipur](https://ekantipur.com/technology/2026/06/14/en/springbaseai-operating-system-launched-in-nepal-22-05.html)
- Nepdev Technology is an agency that builds fixed-scope Messenger/WhatsApp/Viber sales bots in English, Nepali and **Romanised Nepali**. It is the only source naming Romanized Nepali. - [AI Agents Directory](https://aiagentsdirectory.com/agency/nepdev-technology)
- Wiseyak received a GSMA grant (Nov 2025) for conversational AI in English, Nepali and Maithili. - [GSMA](https://www.gsma.com/solutions-and-impact/connectivity-for-good/mobile-for-development/digital-grantees-portfolio/wiseyak/)
- Edesy, an Indian vendor, markets Nepali website and WhatsApp chatbots. Its accuracy claims are unverified. - [Edesy](https://edesy.in/ai-chatbot/countries/nepal)
- Haptik runs Hinglish (Hindi typed in Latin script) as an on-demand BETA. Its blog says missing Hinglish support caused 40-80% of bot breaks for two customers. The pipeline detects the language early and transliterates to Devanagari for understanding. - [Haptik docs](https://docs.haptik.ai/en_US/what-is-a-hinglish-bot); [Haptik blog](https://www.haptik.ai/blog/multilingual-chatbots-hinglish)
- Sarvam voice agents claim support for code-mixed speech. - [Sarvam docs](https://docs.sarvam.ai/voice-agents/pricing)

### Inferences
- Haptik's 40-80% break-rate figure is the strongest public evidence that Romanized input is where South Asian bots fail. Romanized Nepali, handled natively by Boli's own model, is a credible wedge, since no self-serve competitor documents it.

### Gaps
- No pricing, traction or website-widget details were found for TingTing, Nepdev or Edesy. Yellow.ai and Gupshup Hinglish documentation was not found.
