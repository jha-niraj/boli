# Nepal business needs - customer communication and support (as of November 2026)

Researched 2026-11-24. Source quality note: most Nepal-specific numbers on chat volumes, chatbot ROI and COD returns come from VENDOR blogs (chatbot agencies, e-commerce SaaS) and are unsourced. Independent press (Kathmandu Post) and DataReportal cover scale and regulation, not support pain. No Reddit r/Nepal threads or independent merchant surveys turned up in search - treat "real quotes" below as vendor or executive voices, not owner surveys.

## 1. Which channels do Nepali customers use to contact businesses?

### Takeaway
Meta's apps (Facebook page inbox / Messenger, Instagram DM) are the default storefront and inbox for small sellers; Viber is big for person-to-person and surged after the September 2025 social media block; WhatsApp's share is contested (vendors claim it leads, survey-style data says Messenger). TikTok is a growing seller channel. No independent 2026 per-app business-messaging survey exists.

### Cited Findings
- Nepal had 14.8 million social media user identities in October 2025 (50.0% of population) - [DataReportal Digital 2026 Nepal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-nepal); 15.0 million (50.6%) in October 2026 - [DataReportal Digital 2027 Nepal](https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2027-nepal). DataReportal does not break out Messenger on these pages.
- NapoleonCat (ad-planning estimates, inconsistent month labels) gives about 16.5-16.9 million Messenger users in Nepal in Aug-Oct 2026 (about 52% of population) and 17.6-18.4 million Facebook users mid-2026 - [NapoleonCat Messenger Nepal](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/messenger-users-in-nepal/), [NapoleonCat social media Nepal 2026](https://stats.napoleoncat.com/social-media-users-in-nepal/2026/). These exceed DataReportal's totals; not directly comparable.
- An older cross-country dataset lists Facebook Messenger as Nepal's most-used messaging app (13.7M users, 46.1%) - [Ooma](https://www.ooma.com/blog/the-most-popular-messaging-apps-by-country/) (data roughly 500 days old at time of search).
- Viber claims almost 40% market share in Nepal (company figure, old) - [Himalayan Times](https://thehimalayantimes.com/business/we-plan-to-make-viber-the-no-1-messaging-app-in-nepal); after the September 2025 social media ban Viber became the most downloaded app in Nepal, jumping from 89th to 1st - [Nepali Telecom, Sep 2025](https://www.nepalitelecom.com/2025/09/viber-becomes-most-download-app-in-nepal-after-social-media-ban.html).
- During the September 2025 block (lifted about a week later), businesses that took every order through a Facebook page lost contact with customers for days - reported in [Kathmandu Post coverage summary](https://kathmandupost.com/money/2025/07/20/unregistered-online-sellers-now-illegal-in-nepal-under-new-e-commerce-law) and secondary blogs (see search summary; exact blog not verified).
- Vendor claim: over 85% of social commerce transactions in Nepal happen in Messenger and Instagram DMs rather than storefronts - [Sajedar / DEV Community](https://dev.to/dimanjan/architecting-a-2-second-facebook-messenger-ai-chatbot-for-nepal-e-commerce-486). Contradicted on channel order by another vendor claiming WhatsApp carries about 70% of e-commerce customer messages, web widget second, Messenger third, Instagram DM key for fashion - [Praxium Labs, Apr 2026](https://praxiumlabs.com/blog/ecommerce-chatbot-roi-nepal/). Both unsourced.
- Pathao Commerce (launched 2026) built a unified inbox for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok messages; CEO Yashu Thakali: "Our objective is to allow merchants to connect their Facebook pages, Instagram accounts" - [Kathmandu Post, 2026-08-27](https://kathmandupost.com/money/2026/08/27/fast-delivery-changes-the-game-for-nepal-s-online-sellers).
- Thakali (company estimate): social commerce is about 90% of online retail transactions across South Asia and has grown 30-40% a year in Nepal over five years; over 90% of active online merchants are in Kathmandu Valley - [Kathmandu Post, 2026-08-27](https://kathmandupost.com/money/2026/08/27/fast-delivery-changes-the-game-for-nepal-s-online-sellers).
- The E-commerce Act covers sales via Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp; sellers had to register by July 18, 2025 or face up to Rs200,000 fine - [Kathmandu Post, 2025-07-20](https://kathmandupost.com/money/2025/07/20/unregistered-online-sellers-now-illegal-in-nepal-under-new-e-commerce-law). By Aug 2026, 1,530 e-commerce enterprises applied, 345 approved - [Kathmandu Post, 2026-08-27](https://kathmandupost.com/money/2026/08/27/fast-delivery-changes-the-game-for-nepal-s-online-sellers).

### Inferences
- A Messenger/Instagram integration is table stakes for online shops; a website widget alone would miss where the conversations happen. Viber and WhatsApp matter more for services (clinics, consultancies, travel) and person-to-person follow-up.
- Channel concentration on Meta is itself a risk sellers felt in Sept 2025 - a website widget plus Meta inbox gives a fallback story.
- Pathao's unified inbox (2% commission) is a direct competitor for the "one inbox" job among Valley sellers.

### Gaps
- No independent 2025/2026 survey giving % of customers who contact businesses by Messenger vs Viber vs WhatsApp vs phone.
- NTA MIS reports were not retrieved (no OTT app breakdown found).
- TikTok DM usage by businesses unquantified.

## 2. Business owners' complaints (repetitive questions, late-night, staff, slow replies, Romanized Nepali, COD/fake orders)

### Takeaway
The recurring pain set is consistent across sources: the same "price kati?" / "delivery huncha?" questions all day, slow replies at peak evening ad hours and overnight losing sales, manual payment and address work, Romanized Nepali spelling, and COD refusals/fake orders. Almost all of it is vendor-reported; no independent owner survey or Reddit thread was found.

### Cited Findings
- Romanized Nepali examples of real inbox messages: "kati parcha", "delivery charge kasto cha", "L size cha ki chaina", "Dai yo jacket ko price kati ho?" - [Sajedar / DEV Community](https://dev.to/dimanjan/architecting-a-2-second-facebook-messenger-ai-chatbot-for-nepal-e-commerce-486).
- Vendor claim: reply delays exceed 45 minutes during peak ad-spend hours 7-11 PM, linked to abandoned carts; generic no-code bots (ManyChat, Chatfuel) chained with translation take 8-15 seconds - [Sajedar / DEV Community](https://dev.to/dimanjan/architecting-a-2-second-facebook-messenger-ai-chatbot-for-nepal-e-commerce-486).
- DM selling means "answering the same questions all day", typing bank details, checking payment screenshots, copying addresses into courier portals; "Replies slow down when you are busy or asleep," and "customers who wait buy elsewhere." Also names fake COD orders and returned parcels - [Blanxer, page as of Nov 2026](https://www.blanxer.com/sell-on-instagram-facebook-nepal).
- Vendor claim (unsourced): conversion drops about 30% if a pre-purchase question waits more than 30 minutes - [Praxium Labs](https://praxiumlabs.com/blog/ecommerce-chatbot-roi-nepal/).
- Kathmandu customer-support agent costs about NPR 40,000-60,000/month all-in; one agent handles about 200-300 tickets/day - [Praxium Labs](https://praxiumlabs.com/blog/ecommerce-chatbot-roi-nepal/).
- Realistic deflection by ticket type in Nepal: order status 85-95%, return policy 70-85%, product info 60-75%, payment issues 40-60%, complaints 5-15%, negotiation 0-10%; blended 35-60%. Quote: "Real numbers in Nepal are lower (35-60%) but the ROI math still works" - [Praxium Labs](https://praxiumlabs.com/blog/ecommerce-chatbot-roi-nepal/).
- Praxium says a Nepali-language chatbot costs 2-3x the API cost of an English one per conversation (tokenisation) - [Praxium Labs Devanagari guide](https://praxiumlabs.com/blog/devanagari-chatbot-nepali-nlp/) (from search snippet).
- Service-sector pains (vendor): slow replies after hours, staff exhaustion and turnover, inconsistent answers between staff, cold ad leads; unnamed Kathmandu consultancy case with 300+ messages/day and "4 hours/day" counsellor time saved - [EasyGrow Solution, 2026-06-20](https://www.easygrowsolution.com/blog/ai-chatbot-nepal). Unverified case studies.
- COD: vendor claims 30-35% COD return rate and over 85% of deliveries are COD; a Rs100 advance via eSewa/Fonepay QR reportedly cut returns to under 10% - [Sajedar / DEV Community](https://dev.to/dimanjan/architecting-a-2-second-facebook-messenger-ai-chatbot-for-nepal-e-commerce-486). Caution: a Nepal courier article says widely circulated "1% COD" and "50% return" figures appear to come from Bangladesh, and Pathao publishes no Nepal rate card; "when a customer refuses a COD parcel, you pay to send it and to get it back" - [Digital Solution Nepal](https://digitalsolutionnepal.com/nepal-courier-charges-online-sellers/).
- Pathao CEO: "thousands of small sellers lacked an integrated system to streamline their day-to-day operations." - [Kathmandu Post, 2026-08-27](https://kathmandupost.com/money/2026/08/27/fast-delivery-changes-the-game-for-nepal-s-online-sellers).

### Inferences
- The highest-volume, most automatable questions (price, stock/size, delivery charge/area, order status, return policy, payment details) are exactly "answer from the business's own content" - a good fit for a grounded assistant.
- Romanized Nepali understanding is a genuine differentiator vs ManyChat/Chatfuel keyword bots.
- COD-confirmation / advance-payment nudging is a valuable add-on but needs payment-rail integration.

### Gaps
- No Reddit r/Nepal, r/NepalBusiness or press-reported Facebook-group quotes from owners were found.
- No independent COD return-rate data for Nepal (vendor 30-35% figure unverified).
- No independent data on staff turnover in support roles.

## 3. Customer complaints about Nepali businesses' support

### Takeaway
Evidence is anecdotal: unanswered bank mobile-banking support lines, ISP tickets waiting weeks. Formal grievance channels exist (NRB gunaso portal) but no complaint volumes were found.

### Cited Findings
- Trustpilot reviews of Nabil Bank (early 2026) say mobile-banking support calls go unanswered - "the call doesn't even get picked up", 3-5 times - [Trustpilot Nabil Bank](https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/nabilbank.com). Anecdotal.
- Escalation path at banks: grievance officer if no reply within 2 days, then NRB via gunaso.nrb.org.np - [Standard Chartered Nepal contact page](https://www.sc.com/np/contact-us/).
- NRB runs a separate microfinance grievance desk (toll-free 18105000022) - [B360 Nepal](https://b360nepal.com/nrb-reminds-microfinance-customers-of-grievance-hearing-desk-for-complaints).
- NRB directed banks to designate 24-hour contacts to curb cyber fraud - [Republica](https://myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/nrb-directs-banks-to-designate-24-hour-contacts-to-curb-cyber-fraud-42-13.html).
- Bank chatbots exist but are mostly rule-based/menu-driven: Sid (Siddhartha, hybrid, called the only AI-powered one), Viva (Global IME, rule-based, Nepali/English switch), NBL Mitra, Satthi (NIC Asia), Laxmi Didi, Sani (Sanima) - [Swift Technology](https://www.swifttech.com.np/blog/nepali-banks-digital-chat-assistants/); bank bots "neither able to handle many things nor available in Nepali" - [Business 360](https://www.b360nepal.com/detail/9588/ai-ml-opportunities-in-nepal-iii) (older article); Jyoti Bikas Bank launched a multilingual AI chatbot "Jyoti" (2026, date unconfirmed) - [Straits Institute](https://www.straitsai.institute/state-of-applied-ai/nepal).
- Consumer complaints about returns/refunds and fake seller profiles noted (narrated, not quoted) - [Kathmandu Post, 2026-08-27](https://kathmandupost.com/money/2026/08/27/fast-delivery-changes-the-game-for-nepal-s-online-sellers).

### Inferences
- Banks already have chatbots (procurement precedent) but weak Nepali/AI quality - an upgrade opportunity, but long, compliance-heavy sales cycles.

### Gaps
- No NRB complaint counts for FY 2081/82 found; no NTA ISP complaint statistics; no Reddit WorldLink/ISP threads surfaced.
- No data on hospitals, colleges or travel agencies' response times.

## 4. Tools in use and prices

### Takeaway
SMS (Sparrow, NTC bulk) is the established paid channel at roughly NPR 0.5-1.4 per SMS. For chat, owners mostly hand-answer in Meta inboxes, sometimes with Messenger auto-replies or ManyChat/Chatfuel. Local agencies quote NPR 150k-300k builds plus NPR 8k-25k/month, or SaaS at NPR 2,500-12,000/month. Pathao Commerce takes 2% commission for a unified inbox plus ops.

### Cited Findings
- Sparrow SMS festive offer: NPR 0.95/SMS for 5,000, 0.85 for 15,000, 0.75 for 25,000 (pre-VAT); volume-based, no minimum monthly - [Sparrow SMS offer](https://sparrowsms.com/blog/sparrow-sms-bulk-sms-offer/), [Sparrow pricing blog](https://sparrowsms.com/blog/bulk-sms-service-pricing/). Third party: local gateways NPR 0.74-1.40/SMS; NTC bulk NPR 0.50 - [sent.dm](https://www.sent.dm/resources/nepal-sms-pricing).
- Sajedar Messenger AI bot: NPR 2,500-12,000/month volume-based - [Sajedar / DEV Community](https://dev.to/dimanjan/architecting-a-2-second-facebook-messenger-ai-chatbot-for-nepal-e-commerce-486).
- Custom agency build: NPR 150,000-300,000 build; LLM API NPR 1.5-3.5/conversation; WhatsApp messaging NPR 1.4-8.5/conversation; hosting NPR 1,500-3,000/month; retainer NPR 8,000-15,000/month; ongoing NPR 8,000-20,000/month; payback claimed 3-5 months; minimum viable store size about 200 orders/month - [Praxium Labs, Apr 2026](https://praxiumlabs.com/blog/ecommerce-chatbot-roi-nepal/).
- EasyGrow gives no NPR prices ("varies depending on the complexity"); setup 1-2 weeks; channels website, Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp API - [EasyGrow](https://www.easygrowsolution.com/blog/ai-chatbot-nepal).
- Pathao Commerce: unified Facebook/Instagram/TikTok inbox + customer profiles + order history for "a nominal 2 percent commission"; 600+ merchants in two weeks; marketplaces charge 20-25% - [Kathmandu Post, 2026-08-27](https://kathmandupost.com/money/2026/08/27/fast-delivery-changes-the-game-for-nepal-s-online-sellers).
- Local players: Diyo.AI (Nepali/English conversational AI, NAAMII-incubated, founded 2020) - [Sunway College](https://sunway.edu.np/industry-partner/diyo-ai/); TalkFlow ("Nepal's Leading Chatbot Platform for Facebook Messenger", page appears old) - [TalkFlow](https://talkflow.io/); GuffGPT consumer Nepali chat app - [GuffGPT](https://guffgpt.com/); Clutch lists Kathmandu chatbot developers (paid ranking) - [Clutch](https://clutch.co/np/developers/artificial-intelligence/chatbots).
- Sambad.io (chat/inbox product) offered at 30% off to Blanxer customers - [Blanxer](https://www.blanxer.com/sell-on-instagram-facebook-nepal).
- Vendor content for Nepali page auto-reply scripts exists, implying Messenger auto-replies are common - [Sajedar 50 auto-reply samples](https://www.sajedar.com/articles/50-facebook-page-auto-reply-message-samples-nepal-guide).

### Inferences
- NPR 2,500-12,000/month is the current market anchor for a hosted Messenger AI bot; NPR 999/month anchors SMB SaaS (see section 5). A Boli product near NPR 1,500-5,000/month for small shops, higher tiers for institutions, fits observed anchors.
- Agency builds at NPR 150k+ leave room for a self-serve product that is cheaper and faster.

### Gaps
- No verified Nepal pricing for Aakash SMS, TingTing (voice), Tidio/Intercom/Zendesk/Freshdesk adoption, or ManyChat usage share among Nepali pages.
- No independent freelance/agency rate survey for chatbot builds.

## 5. Payment and buying behaviour

### Takeaway
NPR billing via eSewa/Khalti/Fonepay/ConnectIPS is the practical route; foreign SaaS is constrained by dollar-card limits (raised in 2026 but still annual caps). Local SaaS price anchors are low (free tier + NPR 999/month). No evidence found on monthly vs one-time preference or demo/in-person sales need.

### Cited Findings
- Standard dollar-card limit was $500, pushing businesses and developers to informal channels; an April 2026 NRB circular reportedly raised the IT-sector annual limit to $3,000, and up to $5,000 for eligible exporting companies; CAN Federation welcomed it but wants international payment gateways - [ICT Frame](https://ictframe.com/?p=113199), [B360 Nepal](https://www.b360nepal.com/detail/28246/can-federation-welcomes-nrb-directive). Dates in search snippets inconsistent; confirm against NRB Unified Circular 2082.
- Debit card limits cut in Oct 2025 to Rs20,000 per transaction and Rs50,000 per day - [Gadgetbyte Nepal](https://www.gadgetbytenepal.com/nepal-rastra-bank-transaction-limits/).
- Bathuwa (Nepali e-commerce SaaS): free Starter (25 products), Business NPR 999/month, eSewa and Khalti built in - [Bathuwa](https://bathuwa.com/).
- NPR transactions must use NRB-approved domestic gateways (eSewa, Khalti, ConnectIPS) - [Kokil](https://kokil.com.np/blog/ecommerce-payment-gateway-options-for-nepal-compared). Wallet merchant fees quoted as 1.5-2% by one source and 2.5-3.5% by another - [Arjan KC](https://arjankc.com.np/blog/esewa-khalti-fonepay-payment-gateway-nepal/) vs [Kokil](https://kokil.com.np/blog/ecommerce-payment-gateway-options-for-nepal-compared) (conflict).
- Blanxer offers callbacks "within one working day" and uses Fonepay QR - [Blanxer](https://www.blanxer.com/sell-on-instagram-facebook-nepal).

### Inferences
- Pricing in NPR with eSewa/Khalti/Fonepay is effectively required for SMBs; foreign tools (Intercom, Tidio, ManyChat Pro) are hampered by dollar-card limits - a real local wedge.
- Pathao's 2% commission model shows sellers accept % of sales pricing; a flat low monthly fee is the other proven anchor.

### Gaps
- No survey on willingness to pay for SaaS, monthly vs one-time preference, or need for in-person demos. Whether wallets support true auto-recurring billing is unverified.

## 6. Which 3-4 sectors have the sharpest pain and budget first?

### Takeaway
On available evidence: (1) social-commerce shops (highest volume, repetitive questions, Romanized Nepali, evening peaks; low budget but many of them), (2) education/visa consultancies and colleges (high-value leads, 300+ messages/day cited, ad-driven), (3) clinics/hospitals (appointments, FAQs, after-hours), (4) real estate (ad leads going cold). Banks/ISPs have bigger budgets and existing weak bots but long, compliance-heavy sales.

### Cited Findings
- Vendor sector list: educational consultancies (visa, fee, intake questions, booking counselling), real estate (brochures, site visits; NPR 50,000/month ad spend case), clinics/hospitals (appointments, FAQs, reminders), e-commerce (order tracking, product questions) - [EasyGrow, 2026-06-20](https://www.easygrowsolution.com/blog/ai-chatbot-nepal).
- E-commerce volume and Romanized-Nepali pain - [Sajedar](https://dev.to/dimanjan/architecting-a-2-second-facebook-messenger-ai-chatbot-for-nepal-e-commerce-486), [Blanxer](https://www.blanxer.com/sell-on-instagram-facebook-nepal); minimum viable size about 200 orders/month - [Praxium](https://praxiumlabs.com/blog/ecommerce-chatbot-roi-nepal/).
- Banks already deploy mostly rule-based bots with weak Nepali - [Swift Technology](https://www.swifttech.com.np/blog/nepali-banks-digital-chat-assistants/).

### Inferences
- Best first wedge for a "answers from your own content" assistant: consultancies/colleges and clinics (content-heavy FAQs, high value per lead, can pay NPR 5k-15k/month) plus mid-size online shops via Messenger/Instagram (volume, but price-sensitive and competing with Pathao's inbox).
- Banks/fintech/ISPs are a second-phase enterprise play.

### Gaps
- No independent data ranking sectors by message volume or budget; ranking above rests on vendor case studies plus inference.
