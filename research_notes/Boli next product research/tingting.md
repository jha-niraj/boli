# TingTing (tingting.io, a Prixa company) - product, pricing, traction and gaps profile (as of 24 November 2026)

Scope note: the facts marked "own site" were read on tingting.io on 24 Nov 2026. Press facts carry the publication date. Builds on the TingTing section of `reports/Nepal Sarvam style AI platforms.md` (23 Nov 2026), which scored TingTing 5/14 on a Sarvam-style checklist. New in this note: per-product detail, a press price (Rs 2/min), traction numbers, a named customer list, the Dialog Axiata deal (Aug 2026), Omni's channel list and knowledge-base training, the five voices confirmed, and the Riri model family.

## 1. What each product does

### Takeaway
TingTing is a voice-first, campaign-and-call-centre suite for Nepali enterprises: outbound robocalls (Call Broadcast), bulk SMS (Text), voice surveys (Forms), an inbound AI phone agent (Agents), an on-prem AI call centre (Connect), and, since April 2026, a social/web-chat unified inbox with knowledge-base AI replies (Omni). "Connect" is NOT a developer API; it is a call-centre product with an on-prem hardware box. Omni is the only text/chat product and is the closest thing to what Boli would build.

### Cited Findings
- Site lists six products: Call Broadcast, Text, Forms, Agents, Connect, Omni; homepage channels are "voice, SMS, or WhatsApp" — [tingting.io](https://www.tingting.io) (own site)
- **Call Broadcast**: "reach thousands of customers through automated, personalized phone calls in a matter of minutes"; contact data auto-mapped to variables (name, date, amount); upload contacts, write message, launch, "no technical setup, no call center"; real-time analytics, call-status insights, relaunch/re-target; "dedicated carrier-grade SIP lines"; credit calculator. Voice engine Riri "transforms written Nepali and Romanized text into clear, natural speech" — [Call Broadcast page](https://www.tingting.io/product/tingting-call-broadcast) (own site)
- **Text**: "A robust SMS automation solution that helps organizations send marketing, transactional, and OTP messages at scale"; variable personalisation, "dual language support", credit estimation, drafts, scheduling, transactional OTP API, bulk upload — [Text page](https://www.tingting.io/product/tingting-text) (own site). Press: Text is an "SMS engine built on Sparrow SMS technology" — [ICT Samachar, 7 Falgun 2082 (~19 Feb 2026)](https://ictsamachar.com/news/tingting-a-nepali-ai-startup-unifying-business-communication-on-a-single-platform/); "integrated with Nepal's largest SMS infrastructure" — [Nepali Telecom, 1 Jan 2026](https://www.nepalitelecom.com/2026/01/tingting-launches-four-ai-products-via-virtual-product-launch.html)
- **Forms**: automated voice surveys; answers by keypad (DTMF) or speech; personalised variables; "SIP flexibility" — [Forms page](https://www.tingting.io/product/tingting-forms) (own site). Press: converts spoken Nepali answers into structured data — [Nepali Telecom, Jan 2026](https://www.nepalitelecom.com/2026/01/tingting-launches-four-ai-products-via-virtual-product-launch.html)
- **Agents**: inbound-only AI voice agent; "Natural Nepali voice understanding"; trained on the organisation's FAQs; escalates to a human when not confident; "unlimited concurrent calls", 24/7, call analytics; use cases: account registration, KYC, claim status, appointment booking, return policy, loan terms, interest rates; sectors: financial institutions, government, healthcare, retail, e-commerce, cooperatives; integrations not mentioned; cites an unnamed "AI-Powered Election Campaigns" story from a "Head of Information Department" — [Agents page](https://www.tingting.io/product/tingting-agents) (own site)
- **Connect**: "AI-native call center solution... connects Customers -> Support Agents -> AI Intelligence"; every call recorded, transcribed, summarised, sentiment-scored in real time; live dashboards, agent scoring, queue analytics, ticketing, IVR/call-flow builder, AI phone agent; can run fully on-premise via "THE BOX" hardware appliance; flat-cost model with unlimited extensions; waitlist closed, "Get a Free Demo" — [Connect page](https://www.tingting.io/product/tingting-connect) (own site). Launched March 2026, pitched to banks and government as "Sovereign AI", claims 100% of conversations analysed vs ~2% manual QA — [Bajarko Chirfar, 3 Mar 2026](https://eng.bajarkochirfar.com/2026/03/03/tingting-tingting-launches-nepals-first-ai-native-call-center-solution/)
- **Omni**: "Manage every customer conversation no matter where it comes from"; channels WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, Email, Website Chat, TikTok, "6+ channels"; AI auto-replies generated from the business's knowledge base; co-pilot mode (AI suggests, human approves); human takeover; ticketing with resolution-time tracking; tags. Knowledge base trains on PDFs, manuals, spreadsheets, FAQ and marketing web pages, public Google Docs; importing history from Gmail/Facebook/WhatsApp is "Soon". No languages, prices or customers listed — [Omni page](https://www.tingting.io/product/tingting-omni) (own site). Launched April 2026 — [About](https://www.tingting.io/about) (own site)
- Omni channel list conflicts: press says Omni merges "WhatsApp, Viber, Messenger, Webchat, and SMS" — [Headline Nepal, ~22 Aug 2026](https://english.headlinenepal.com/details/48055); own site lists TikTok/Instagram/Email and no Viber or SMS — [Omni page](https://www.tingting.io/product/tingting-omni)
- Omni has an iOS app ("Ting Ting Omni", seller Prixa Technologies) with no ratings yet — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ting-ting-omni/id6759367107)
- **Experience Center**: try-it demos for Call Broadcast (KYC reminders, OTP awareness, cross-sell, bill reminders, follow-ups), Text (KYC, delivery updates, OTP, healthcare appointments, bill payment), Forms (bank CSAT, food delivery review, insurance continuation, course admission); a 24/7 phone number to talk to the AI agent; an Omni interactive tour with no sign-up. Five voices: Ashmita (preferred female), Subina (premium female), Samriddhi (expressive female), Dhiraj (male, beta), Binod (preferred male) — [Experience Center](https://www.tingting.io/experience-center) (own site). This resolves the 23 Nov report's "five voices unconfirmed" note: five voices are confirmed.
- Models: "Riri" family - Riri-1 TTS (also used by news portals and government sites), Riri-2 speech-to-text, Riri-3 "generative AI for two-way customer conversations"; "all technologies built from scratch for Nepali"; each voice needs 10-20 hours of clean audio — [ICT Samachar, Feb 2026](https://ictsamachar.com/news/tingting-a-nepali-ai-startup-unifying-business-communication-on-a-single-platform/). Riri One TTS first launched by Prixa on Annapurna Post (LinkedIn post ~early 2023) — [Prixa LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/prixa_riri-one-nepals-first-lifelike-text-to-speech-activity-7028578037738733568-b3Zb). Claims understanding of intent, tone and emotion in under 200 ms — [Nepali Telecom, Jan 2026](https://www.nepalitelecom.com/2026/01/tingting-launches-four-ai-products-via-virtual-product-launch.html)
- Languages: Nepali + Romanized Nepali input for TTS (own product pages); Nepali and English scripts and calls — [Gadgetbyte, 5 Jun 2025](https://www.gadgetbytenepal.com/tingting/); Nepali, English and Sinhala available, Tamil/Sinhala voice models in development — [Headline Nepal, ~Aug 2026](https://english.headlinenepal.com/details/48055)

### Inferences
- The suite is built around the phone call. Omni is the only text-first product, and it is an inbox for support teams, not a model or API a developer can call.
- "Riri-3" generative AI is probably what powers Agents and Omni auto-replies, but nothing states whether it is an in-house LLM or a wrapper over a third-party model. The Omni features (RAG on PDFs and web pages, co-pilot) look like a standard LLM + retrieval product; given the team's size and history (TTS-first), a third-party LLM underneath is plausible but unverified.

### Gaps
- No source says which LLM powers Omni or Agents, or whether Omni replies in Romanized Nepali.
- Omni's real channel list is inconsistent between site and press; Viber support unconfirmed.
- No Omni pricing anywhere.

## 2. Pricing, trial and signup

### Takeaway
No public price list on the site. Signup is self-serve with 100 free AI credits (no card); credits are defined but no NPR price per credit is published. Press reports Rs 2 per minute for calls. Agents and Connect are sales-led (contact for price; Connect is flat-cost, unlimited extensions).

### Cited Findings
- "100 free AI credits to be used across TingTing Suite - no card required"; Login/Signup at suite.tingting.io; no pricing page — [tingting.io](https://www.tingting.io); [pricing URL returns no prices](https://www.tingting.io/pricing) (own site)
- Credit rules: 1 voice credit = 30 s of call time (billed in 30 s steps); 1 SMS credit = one message within the character limit; surveys 3 credits per call; AI inbound calls 5 credits; credits apply to Call Broadcast, Text, Forms; "For TingTing Agents and TingTing Connect, please contact our team directly for pricing" — [FAQs](https://www.tingting.io/faqs) (own site)
- Press price: Rs 2 per minute (Rs 1 for connection, Rs 1 after the system starts speaking); claims global cloud telephony costs Rs 30-40 per call — [ICT Samachar, ~19 Feb 2026](https://ictsamachar.com/news/tingting-a-nepali-ai-startup-unifying-business-communication-on-a-single-platform/)
- Connect: "flat-cost model with unlimited extensions" — [Connect page](https://www.tingting.io/product/tingting-connect); press called it "zero cost" with no per-seat or license fees — [Bajarko Chirfar, Mar 2026](https://eng.bajarkochirfar.com/2026/03/03/tingting-tingting-launches-nepals-first-ai-native-call-center-solution/)
- Contact form asks company name, industry, company size; "Talk to Sales" and "Book a Demo" on every page — [Contact](https://www.tingting.io/contact)

### Inferences
- Self-serve covers the campaign products; anything AI-conversational (Agents, Connect, probably Omni beyond the trial) is sales-led. A developer cannot see what a month will cost before talking to sales.

### Gaps
- No NPR price per credit, per SMS, or for Omni; Rs 2/min is press-reported only and may be out of date.

## 6. Gaps a developer-first open-model text/chat platform (Boli) could fill

### Takeaway
TingTing owns enterprise voice campaigns and phone agents in Nepal and has strong customer logos, telco/SMS plumbing and a Sri Lanka telco partner. It leaves the developer text layer empty: no chat/completions API, no published prices, no embeddable model, no docs worth the name. Boli should not fight on voice campaigns; it should be the model/API layer, possibly even a supplier to Omni-style products.

### Cited Findings (evidence behind each gap)
- No LLM/chat endpoint, SDK, webhooks or rate limits; docs partly copied from Modal — [For developers](https://www.tingting.io/for-developers)
- No published NPR price per credit; Agents/Connect "contact our team" — [FAQs](https://www.tingting.io/faqs)
- Omni language support unstated; knowledge base import still "Soon" for history — [Omni page](https://www.tingting.io/product/tingting-omni)
- Model provenance for generative replies undisclosed — [Blog, Mar 2026](https://www.tingting.io/blogs/tingting-next-communication-for-understanding); blog index has no posts listed — [Blogs](https://www.tingting.io/blogs)

### Inferences (Boli opportunities)
1. **OpenAI-compatible Nepali chat API with public NPR prices and self-serve keys** - nothing like it from TingTing. Developers at banks, ISPs, wallets (TingTing's own customers) building in-app assistants have no Nepali text API to call.
2. **Romanized Nepali and code-mixed text** as a stated, tested feature. TingTing handles Romanized input only for TTS; chat handling is unstated.
3. **Embeddable website chat widget / RAG-over-your-docs for SMEs** at a self-serve price. Omni does this inside a sales-led inbox suite; a lighter, cheaper "answer from my site/PDFs" widget with a free tier targets the long tail (schools, clinics, shops, cooperatives) TingTing's enterprise motion skips.
4. **Open models and data sovereignty with transparency**: TingTing sells sovereignty via on-prem hardware (THE BOX) for big buyers. Boli can offer named open weights (Gemma 4 E4B) and self-hostable models for mid-size buyers and developers, with clear model cards - TingTing discloses nothing about its LLM.
5. **Real developer experience**: docs, SDK samples, playground (Kura), usage dashboard, webhooks. TingTing's docs are a list of endpoint names.
6. **Text utilities around SMS**: TingTing Text sends templated SMS; Boli could offer generation, Nepali/English rewriting, Preeti/Unicode conversion, transliteration, classification of inbound replies - as API calls any SMS sender (including Sparrow-based ones) can use.
7. **Possible partner, not rival**: Omni/Agents need a Nepali LLM; if Riri-3 is weak or third-party, Boli's API could be a supplier. Worth a conversation later.

Risks: TingTing has distribution (50+ enterprise clients, eSewa, WorldLink, Dialog Axiata) and could expose an Omni/Riri-3 API quickly; it already has self-serve signup and free credits.

### Gaps
- No hands-on test of Omni's AI reply quality in Nepali/Romanized Nepali (would need signup); no data on how many SMEs use TingTing self-serve vs enterprise.
