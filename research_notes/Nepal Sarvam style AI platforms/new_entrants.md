# New or previously missed Nepal-based self-serve AI platforms (as of 23 Nov 2026)

Scope: Nepal-based companies, labs, telcos or government efforts offering developer AI APIs or a playground, excluding the known list (TingTing/Prixa, Koili.ai, Ampixa Labs, Naamche Labs, TalkC.ai, Wiseyak/WiseAI, Paaila Technology, Diyo AI, Fusemachines, Kathmandu AI Labs, NAAMII, NConnect AI). Research ran on 23 Nov 2026, reading public web pages only.

## Has any Nepal-based self-serve AI/speech API platform launched in 2026, especially since October 2026?

### Takeaway
I found no Nepal-based platform that matches Sarvam's model (public signup, playground, API keys, public docs and public pricing) for Nepali speech, and none that launched in October or November 2026. The closest new finds have API docs, but access is by invitation or waitlist: CoreValley AI (an inference cloud with OpenAI-compatible endpoints), YetiCloud.ai (GPU cloud) and Awaj AI / Ekghanti AwajAI (a Nepali voice API with a waitlist).

### Cited Findings
New candidates not on the known list:

- **CoreValley AI Pvt. Ltd. (Kathmandu)** - [corevalley.ai](https://corevalley.ai/)
  - Calls itself "Nepal's own AI cloud", with its datacentre in Nepal and data kept in the country.
  - Offers hosted models via serverless APIs, managed inference, fine-tuning, a managed AI workspace, MLOps tools and GPU rental (NVIDIA H200 NVL, plus H100, L40S, L4 and RTX PRO 6000 listed).
  - Lists use cases for Nepali speech-to-text and Nepali-language call answering, and a "language AI" offering for Nepali and other Nepal languages. It does not name specific hosted STT/TTS models.
  - Signup: no self-serve signup. Access is through a "Request access" form.
  - Playground: none mentioned.
  - Pricing: a pricing page exists (per-second billing, paid in NPR, no contract), but rates are "being finalised" and customers get quotes.
  - Launch date: not stated. The page shows © 2026 and footnotes dated 13 Oct 2026.
  - [corevalley.ai](https://corevalley.ai/)
- **CoreValley API docs** - [docs.corevalley.ai](https://docs.corevalley.ai/)
  - The docs are public and cover GPU pods, JupyterHub, "Model endpoints & API" (described as "OpenAI-compatible endpoints" controlled by API keys), billing in NPR and a quickstart.
  - No hosted model list and no prices are given.
  - Early access is "open to enterprises on NVIDIA H200", by emailing info@corevalley.ai.
  - RTX PRO 6000 and L40S are marked "coming soon".
  - [docs.corevalley.ai](https://docs.corevalley.ai/)
- **Awaj AI** - [awaj.ai](https://awaj.ai/)
  - Lists Talk with AI, Personal Voice Clone, Multilingual Voice (TTS+STT), Audio Agents, AI Interviews and AI Call Center Support.
  - The homepage does not name Nepal or the company location. A search snippet of the same site said it "provides enterprise-grade native Nepali Voice APIs including Text-to-Speech (TTS), Speech-to-Text (STT), and Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)" ([search result for awaj.ai](https://awaj.ai/)).
  - Signup: waitlist only ("Notify me"). The next version is "almost here" after a "long private beta".
  - No playground, no public docs, no public pricing and no date.
  - [awaj.ai](https://awaj.ai/)
- **Ekghanti AwajAI** - [ekghanti.com.np/awaj-ai](https://ekghanti.com.np/awaj-ai/)
  - Ekghanti (a .com.np company) has a page titled "AwajAI - Omnichannel Telephony & Call Center Solutions" in search results. The page returned HTTP 404 when fetched on 23 Nov 2026.

### Inferences
- The only Nepal-based players with public developer documentation in 2026 that I could find are infrastructure clouds: CoreValley (OpenAI-compatible endpoints) and YetiCloud. Neither publishes hosted Nepali speech models, a playground or a rate card. A Sarvam-style self-serve Nepali speech API from a Nepal company still appears to be an open gap as of Nov 2026.
- Awaj AI is the most direct new speech-API competitor, but it is pre-launch with a waitlist. It is probably connected to Ekghanti, based on the shared "AwajAI" name and Ekghanti's Nepali domain, but I have not confirmed this.
- CoreValley's footnotes are dated 13 Oct 2026, so its public site may have gone live or been updated in October 2026. That would make it the only plausible "since October 2026" entrant, but the launch date is unconfirmed.

### Gaps
- CoreValley's founding date, founders, actual hosted models and whether any Nepali STT/TTS model is live could not be found.
- I could not confirm the company behind Awaj AI, its location or its launch date. The Ekghanti page returned 404.
- No search surfaced Product Hunt launches of Nepal-based AI APIs in 2026. I did not query Hugging Face or GitHub org listings directly.
- Searches did not surface direct Techpana, Techlekh or ICTByte articles on new Nepali voice API launches for Oct-Nov 2026. Local press coverage may exist that search did not index.

## What did Bhashini x KU actually deliver so far?

### Takeaway
Nothing public has shipped. I found no Nepali voice platform, API, dataset release or pilot from Bhashini x Kathmandu University's Centre for DPI & AI as of 23 Nov 2026. The only public outputs are the 6 Jun 2026 MoU and a reported round of exploratory discussions.

### Cited Findings
- The MoU was signed in Mumbai on 6 Jun 2026 by Amitabh Nag (CEO, Digital India BHASHINI Division) and Prof. Bal Krishna Bal (Associate Dean, KU). Its stated purpose is to co-create a "voice first" language translation DPI for Nepal. Planned scope covers Nepali datasets, speech corpora, STT, TTS, machine translation and conversational AI, to help the Government of Nepal deliver services in citizens' languages - [ANI](https://aninews.in/news/national/general-news/digital-india-bhashini-signs-mou-with-kathmandu-university-for-co-creating-national-digital-infrastructure-for-voice-first-language-translation-platform-for-nepal20260607173939/); [FintechBizNews](https://www.fintechbiznews.com/fintech-technology/bhashini-kathmandu-university-to-create-voice-first-platform-for-nepal); [OpenGov Asia](https://opengovasia.com/india-and-nepal-partner-on-multilingual-ai-and-digital-public-infrastructure/?c=us)
- A report datelined New Delhi, 2 Sep 2026, describes discussions between BHASHINI and KU's Centre for DPI & AI, followed by a meeting with the Vice Chancellor. Topics were models for Nepali, Newari, Tamang and Gurung; the Prachalit and Ranjana scripts; datasets; compute; and voice-based service access. The article mentions no pilot, platform, API or dataset release - [APAC News Network](https://apacnewsnetwork.com/2026/09/bhashini-and-kathmandu-university-discuss-language-ai-for-nepal/); [Digital India Times](https://digitalindiatimes.com/digital-india-bhashini-kathmandu-university-explore-collaboration-in-language-ai/)
- A Nepal News piece of 30 Jun 2026 argues that Nepali-language AI is "stuck at the door". It notes that Nepal has no government-level platform like India's Bhashini - [Nepal News](https://nepalnews.com/2026/06/30/nepali-language-stuck-at-the-door-of-ai/)

### Inferences
- The Sep 2026 "explore collaboration" framing, three months after a signed MoU, suggests the work is still at scoping stage. The order of the sources is also odd: the "discussions" report is dated after the MoU, so the APAC piece may be a republished account of earlier talks.
- Any Nepali models would probably appear first on Bhashini's own platform as Nepali language support rather than as a separate Nepal-branded API. I did not check this.

### Gaps
- I did not fetch Bhashini's official site, its model catalogue (to see whether Nepali ASR/TTS is listed) or KU's Centre for DPI & AI pages. That was not possible within the tool budget.
- I found no source dated Oct-Nov 2026 on the MoU's progress.

## Are there Nepali GPU cloud / inference hosting companies (data centres offering AI inference)?

### Takeaway
Yes, two are now public, plus a government plan:
- **YetiCloud.ai** (DataHub with US-based Hosted AI) launched on 3 Jul 2026. It is billed as Nepal's first GPU-as-a-Service and has no public rate card.
- **CoreValley AI** (Kathmandu) is an H200-based "sovereign AI cloud" with OpenAI-compatible model endpoints. Access is invite-only.
- The government's Syuchatar Sovereign AI Compute Centre is budgeted, but has no specs, operator or timeline.

### Cited Findings
- **YetiCloud.ai launch.** DataHub launched it on 3 Jul 2026 at the Marriott, Kathmandu, as "Nepal's first GPU-as-a-Service".
  - GPU model, count and rate card were not disclosed, and the claim that it is "cheaper than US clouds" is unverified.
  - Access had not opened when the article was written, and a keynote speaker conceded "It's probably also not 99.99 uptime".
  - [TechSansar](https://techsansar.com/computing/yeticloud-ai-nepal-sovereign-compute/)
- **YetiCloud.ai capacity.** It reportedly has about 12 GPUs at launch, with more expected, and its website was not accessible at the time of writing - [DCD](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/datahub-launches-ai-cloud-in-nepal-in-partnership-with-hosted-ai/); [NepaliTelecom](https://www.nepalitelecom.com/yeticloud-ai-launched-nepal); [IDC Nova](https://www.idcnova.com/news/DataHub-and-Hosted-AI-Launch-YetiCloud.ai-an-AI-Cloud-Offering-Powered-by-Nepals-Hydropower-6418)
- **DataHub's Yeti Cloud page.**
  - It has a "Sign Up Now" link to app.yetiapp.cloud.
  - Pricing is usage-based and paid in NPR, with no rates shown.
  - GPUaaS is listed separately with no specs.
  - There is no inference API or docs (the tutorials link is a placeholder). The page shows © 2026.
  - [DataHub](https://datahub.com.np/yeti-cloud/)
- **CoreValley AI Pvt. Ltd.** It describes H200 NVL GPUs, model endpoints, per-second billing in NPR and invite-only early access for enterprises - [corevalley.ai](https://corevalley.ai/); [docs.corevalley.ai](https://docs.corevalley.ai/)
- **Syuchatar Sovereign AI Compute Centre.**
  - Announced in the FY 2083/84 budget (29 May 2026) with no specs, funding line, timeline or operator.
  - The DoIT Director General said the government wants to be an enabler, not a competitor, and that the site is not fixed.
  - [TechSansar](https://techsansar.com/computing/yeticloud-ai-nepal-sovereign-compute/); [aggregator citing Kathmandu Post/Ratopati/ShareSansar](https://nepalinews.sanjayasubedi.com.np/worldevents/4c13727c-ef41-4011-a460-af8cfad8dcde/)
  - Experts question its feasibility: "To build a data center with thousands of GPUs, we need billions of dollars" - [Nepalnews](https://english.nepalnews.com/s/feature/nepals-ai-compute-center-plan-draws-scrutiny-over-feasibility-and-risks/)
- **Bichuten Data Vault.** It reportedly announced plans for two AI-capable data centres (5 MW combined), but I did not open the primary source (search summary only) - [DCD search context](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/datahub-launches-ai-cloud-in-nepal-in-partnership-with-hosted-ai/)

### Inferences
- CoreValley is the closest thing in Nepal to an inference API provider, because it has OpenAI-compatible endpoints and API keys. A Nepali speech startup could host models there to keep data in Nepal, but neither cloud offers ready-made Nepali speech APIs.

### Gaps
- No public GPU-hour prices for either provider.
- I could not confirm whether YetiCloud.ai's site is live now (Nov 2026).
- I did not verify the Bichuten Data Vault details or the timing of its plans.

## What Nepali AI products launched at events, hackathons, Product Hunt, or in press during 2026? (including government, NT and Ncell)

### Takeaway
2026 press shows mostly consumer apps, telco chatbots and policy or infrastructure moves. There were no developer-facing Nepali AI APIs. Nepal Telecom and Ncell have text chatbots but no AI developer products. The government's National AI Centre exists under the 2025 policy, but has shipped no public AI platform.

### Cited Findings
- **Nepal Telecom.** It soft-launched an AI "NT Chatbot" for customer queries. The page is dated 3 Sep 2026 but also shows "905 days ago", so the date is unclear. No voice capability - [NepaliTelecom](https://www.nepalitelecom.com/nepal-telecom-nt-chatbot)
- **Ncell.** It runs the "MAYA" chatbot on Messenger, Viber, WhatsApp and its website. This is older and not voice - [Ncell](https://www.ncell.com.np/en/about/media-room/press-release/ncell-launches-chatbot-service-maya)
  - On 19 Nov 2026 Ncell signed an MoU with Samsung Nepal on Galaxy AI access and device bundles. This is not an AI API - [NepaliTelecom](https://www.nepalitelecom.com/2026/11/samsung-ncell-partner-to-expand-ai-access-promote-4g-upgrades-and-galaxy-phones.html); [Ratopati](https://english.ratopati.com/story/87286/samsung-nepal-and-ncell-partner-to-expand-ai-access-and-drive-4g-transition)
- **National AI Policy 2025.**
  - Approved by Cabinet in Aug 2025. It mandates a National AI Centre under the Ministry of Communication that also serves as secretariat to the AI Regulation Council - [Official policy PDF](https://giwmscdnone.gov.np/media/pdf_upload/National%20AI%20Policy-Final_uxc94vg.pdf)
  - The Centre was reportedly inaugurated in Nov 2025. MoCIT was restructured on 13 May 2026 under the Work Division Regulations 2083 - [AI Policy Tracker](https://aipolicytracker.org/policies/nepal-national-ai-policy); [nepalailabs.com explainer](https://nepalailabs.com/nepal-ai-policy)
  - I found no report confirming that the Regulation Council is operating, and there is no AI statute.
- **Judiciary ICT Policy 2026 (about 3 Jul 2026).** It allows AI for legal research and administration, but not for writing judgments - [aggregator citing Kathmandu Post](https://nepalinews.sanjayasubedi.com.np/worldevents/4c13727c-ef41-4011-a460-af8cfad8dcde/)
- **Consumer product: Hajur AI** (1 Mar 2026), a Nepali chatbot inside a super-app - [Janakhabar](https://janakhabar.com/news-and-technology/2026/03/07/119339/)
- **Funding: Lamina Labs** (founded by Nepali students, based in San Francisco, so out of geographic scope). It raised $3M for its video explainer "Simi", which supports Nepali among 80 languages - [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/feed/nepali-ai-startup-lamina-labs-secures-3m-for-video-explainer-tool-simi)
- **Events.** Nepali teams won prizes at Codeavour 7.0, an AI, coding and robotics competition in Jakarta, in early June 2026 - [aggregator citing Ratopati/ShareSansar](https://nepalinews.sanjayasubedi.com.np/worldevents/4c13727c-ef41-4011-a460-af8cfad8dcde/)
- **Infrastructure event.** The YetiCloud.ai launch event was held on 3 Jul 2026 - [TechSansar](https://techsansar.com/computing/yeticloud-ai-nepal-sovereign-compute/)

### Inferences
- The telcos are AI consumers (chatbots, device bundles), not AI API providers. Neither is a likely Sarvam-style competitor in the near term.
- Government activity is policy and compute planning. A government Nepali language platform does not yet exist, and Bhashini x KU is the only stated route to one.

### Gaps
- I did not find any Nepal-based AI API launched on Product Hunt in 2026.
- I could not reliably date the NT Chatbot soft launch.
- I found no Oct-Nov 2026 hackathon outcomes (for example NAAMII or Locus events) that produced public APIs.
- I did not verify the National AI Centre's 2026 activity from a primary government source.
