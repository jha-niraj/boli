The public site (bolihq.com) and the app (app.bolihq.com) are now separate. The site gains a [blog](/blog), [guides](/guides), this changelog and [model pages](/models), with a new menu that groups products, developers, resources and company. Sign-in, Kura, keys and billing stay in the app, and the API's base URL is `https://app.bolihq.com/v1`.
