Every account is now a workspace. Credits, API keys and Kura chats belong to the workspace, and you can invite people to it as members and choose which products each of them can use. If you belong to more than one workspace, switch between them at the top of the rail.

The app now has a product rail on the left: Kura, API and Jawaf, with Kagaj (documents) and Swar (speech) marked as coming later soon. Each product shows only its own pages, and your Kura chat history moved into Kura's sidebar. Credits, billing and your profile sit at the bottom of the rail.

Jawaf is in pilot with a small group of businesses. It answers a business's customers from its own website, files and facts, in Nepali, Romanized Nepali or English - through a link and QR poster, a chat bubble on the website, or the API. When it does not know, it says so and takes a phone number. Want it for your business? [Join the pilot](/contact?topic=Jawaf).
