Most people in Nepal do not type Nepali in Devanagari. They type it in Latin letters - *mero exam sakiyo, aba k garne?* - and they mix in English mid-sentence. Boli starts there: an open model that reads how people actually write, a chat app to talk to it, and an API to build with it, priced in Nepali rupees.

## What you can use today

**<Fact name="kura" />** is the chat. Sign up, verify your email and you get <Fact name="welcomeCredits" /> free credits to talk to the model in Romanized Nepali, English, Devanagari or any mix of them. It answers in the language and script you wrote in.

**The API** is the same model behind an OpenAI-compatible endpoint. If you have called OpenAI before, you change two things: the base URL and the key. The official OpenAI SDKs for Python, TypeScript, JavaScript and Go work as they are.

```bash
curl https://app.bolihq.com/v1/chat/completions \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $BOLI_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"model": "boli-chat", "messages": [{"role": "user", "content": "mero exam sakiyo, aba k garne?"}]}'
```

The [quickstart](/docs/quickstart) gets you from nothing to a working call in about five minutes.

## The model

Boli runs **<Fact name="model" />**, which is <Fact name="modelLabel" />: open weights under <Fact name="licence" />, with <Fact name="context" /> tokens of context. We chose it after putting two small open models through the same test of real Romanized Nepali, English and mixed messages, with everything but the model held fixed. It understood Romanized Nepali where the other often did not, and it stayed in the user's script more often. The [model page](/models/boli-chat) has the details, including what it is not good at yet.

Boli runs only open models. No closed model sits behind the API or behind <Fact name="kura" />.

## What we keep and what we do not

API calls store metadata only: the model, the token counts, the cost, the time and the status. Never your prompts and never the answers. <Fact name="kura" /> conversations are saved, because they are yours and you want them back.

## What comes next

Chat is the first step. Next is document intelligence: reading scanned bills, forms and notices in Nepali and English. After that, speech. Each one ships when an open model passes our own tests on real Nepali input. The [roadmap](/models#roadmap) says where each one stands.
